jobPilot — ten design directions

Same product, same real job records from the W0 scrape, ten different answers. Every direction is a clickable mockup with all twelve P0 screens. Nothing here is product code: the chosen direction gets rebuilt properly in P1.

P0 gate — pick one, or mix parts 12 screens each — dashboard, jobs, triage, verification, doctor, logs, scope, agent setup, credentials, data, onboarding, PWA PC + PWA — resize to 390px wide to see the phone layout

Review checklist — used to keep the ten genuinely distinct

  • Navigation model differs, not just the colour
  • Density differs: rows per screen varies 3×
  • Typeface family and scale differ
  • Radius and border treatment differ
  • Dashboard answers a different question first
  • Verification wall is unreachable by accident in all ten
  • Status is never colour-only
  • Works at 390px without horizontal scroll
  • Keyboard focus visible
  • No direction is a recolour of another

Every direction has twelve screens: append #dashboard, #jobs, #triage, #verification, #doctor, #logs, #scope, #agent, #credentials, #data, #onboarding or #pwa to its address. Resize the window to 390px to see the phone layout. Buttons only raise a “not wired in P0” notice. The comparison, recommendation, tokens and component inventory live in DESIGN-DECISION.md.
